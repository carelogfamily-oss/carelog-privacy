CareLog Support

For app questions, purchase issues, bug reports, or privacy inquiries, email carelogfamily@gmail.com.

If CareLog cannot open your local health records, do not immediately uninstall the app. Your data may still be on the device, and uninstalling could remove it.

When reporting a problem, include your iOS version and what happened. Please avoid sending names, diagnoses, medication lists, or other sensitive health information unless essential to resolving your request.

For subscription management, open iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.
