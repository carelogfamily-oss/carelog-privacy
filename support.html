Support

Need help with Havlori? For app questions, purchase issues, bug reports, or privacy inquiries, email carelogfamily@gmail.com.

Reporting a problem

Tell us which iPhone or iPad model and iOS version you use, which screen was open, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. If useful, include the Havlori app version. Please avoid emailing names, diagnoses, medication lists, or other sensitive health information.

Medication reminders

Check that reminders are enabled in Havlori and that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings. Focus modes and notification preview settings can affect when and how alerts appear. Reminder times follow the device's local time, including when its time zone changes. Do not rely on app notifications as your only method for essential medication tasks.

Premium and subscription management

Open Havlori → Settings → Premium to check your subscription status. If Premium is active, use Manage Subscription. You can also manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions.

If you previously purchased Premium but the app does not recognize an active subscription, open Upgrade to Premium and use Restore Purchases. Purchases must be associated with the Apple Account used for the original subscription. Restoring purchases cannot reactivate an expired subscription.

Protecting local records

Havlori stores entered health records on your device and does not currently provide a separate cloud synchronization or full-data restoration service. If you cannot open your records, do not immediately uninstall the app: deleting it can erase local records. Contact support before taking potentially destructive troubleshooting steps.
