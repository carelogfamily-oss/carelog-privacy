Havlori — Family Health Organizer for iPhone

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Havlori helps families organize health information while keeping them in control of the details they enter. This policy explains how the current version of the app handles that information. Havlori is an organizational tool, not a healthcare provider.

Information you add

Havlori lets you store family profile names and ages, health notes, medications and dosages, appointments, reminders, chronic conditions, and allergies. You choose what to enter. Only add or share another person's information when you are authorized to do so.

How your data is stored

The health information you enter is stored in Havlori's private app storage on your device. No Havlori account is required. Havlori does not upload these records to developer-operated servers. Depending on your settings, Apple's system backups may include app data; Havlori does not control those backups.

Medication reminders and notifications

If you enable medication reminders, Havlori schedules local iOS notifications on your device. Depending on your notification preview settings, a reminder may display the family profile name and a generic medication reminder on your Lock Screen. You can manage notification permission and previews in iPhone Settings.

Notifications depend on iOS settings and device conditions and should not be your only method of remembering essential medication tasks.

Doctor visit summaries and sharing

When you export a doctor visit summary, Havlori creates a protected temporary PDF on your device and removes its temporary copy after the sharing sheet closes. You decide whether and where to share it. Copies you save or send through Files, Mail, Messages, or other apps are controlled by you and those services, not by Havlori. Check the recipient before sharing sensitive information.

Third-party services and subscriptions

This version does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking, or AI services. Apple processes subscription purchases through StoreKit under Apple's privacy practices. If you voluntarily contact support by email, the information you include is handled by your email provider and the support mailbox. Please avoid sending medication lists or other medical details unless necessary.

Retention, deletion, and backups

You can remove a profile and its associated records in Havlori. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data from that device. This does not delete exported documents, messages you have shared, or copies retained in Apple-managed device backups. Manage those separately in the relevant apps or Apple settings.

Havlori does not currently provide its own cloud synchronization or full-data restoration service. If your device is lost, replaced, or reset, records may be unrecoverable unless a compatible device backup is available. Do not uninstall Havlori to troubleshoot a data-access issue without considering the risk of losing local records.

Children's privacy

Havlori is intended for adult caregivers and families, not for independent use by children. Do not enter another person's information unless you are authorized to manage or share it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Havlori changes. Material changes to how information is handled will be reflected in an updated policy and last-updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, email carelogfamily@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive medical records in your initial message.

Havlori does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions.
