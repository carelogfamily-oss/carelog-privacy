CareLog Privacy Policy

CareLog is designed to help families organize health information while keeping users in control of that information.

Information You Add

CareLog lets you store information such as names, age, health notes, medications, appointments, reminders, chronic conditions, and allergies.

How Your Data Is Stored

Your CareLog information is currently stored locally on your device. CareLog does not currently require an account and does not send your health information to CareLog servers.

Notifications

If you enable medication reminders, CareLog uses Apple's local notification system to schedule reminders on your device. Notification permission can be changed at any time in iPhone Settings.

Sharing

CareLog allows you to create and share a doctor visit summary. Sharing happens only when you choose to use the Share feature. The destination you select, such as Messages, Mail, or another app, may have its own privacy practices.

Third-Party Services

The current version of CareLog does not use advertising networks, analytics services, or AI services to collect or process your health information.

Deleting Your Information

You can delete information you have entered in CareLog, including profiles and their associated health information. Because CareLog currently stores this information locally, deleting the app from your device also removes its locally stored app data.

Children's Privacy

CareLog is designed for use by adult caregivers and family members. It is not intended for independent use by children. Information about a child should only be added by a parent, guardian, or other authorized caregiver.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as CareLog adds or changes features. Material changes to how information is handled will be reflected in an updated version of this policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or CareLog, contact us at carelogfamily@gmail.com .

Health Disclaimer

CareLog is an organizational tool and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical decisions.
