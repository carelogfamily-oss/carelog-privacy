CareLog — Family Health Organizer for iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

CareLog is an organizational app that helps caregivers keep medications, health notes, appointments, and doctor visit information together. This policy explains how the current version handles the information you enter. CareLog is not a medical provider or a diagnosis or treatment service.

Information you enter

CareLog can store profile names and ages, medications and dosages, appointment details, health notes, chronic conditions, allergies, and medication reminders. You decide which information to enter. Only record another person's information if you are authorized to manage it.

Where your information is stored

The records you enter are stored in the app's private storage on your device. You do not need a CareLog account, and CareLog does not upload these records to developer-operated servers. Depending on your device settings, Apple's system backups may include app data. CareLog does not control Apple's backups or their retention.

Notifications

If you enable medication reminders, CareLog uses local iOS notifications. A notification may show a medication name on your Lock Screen, depending on your preview settings. You can change notification permission and previews in iPhone Settings.

Doctor visit summaries and sharing

When you export a doctor visit summary, CareLog creates a protected temporary PDF on the device and removes its temporary copy after the system sharing sheet closes. You choose the recipients or destinations. Copies saved to Files, Mail, Messages, or other apps are then subject to those apps' and recipients' privacy practices. Check the recipient before sending sensitive information.

Third-party services and subscriptions

This version does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising networks, tracking technologies, or AI services. Subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit, under Apple's privacy practices. If you voluntarily email CareLog support, the information you include is handled by your email provider and the support mailbox. Please avoid emailing medical details.

Retention, deletion, and backups

You can delete a profile and its associated records inside CareLog. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data from that device. This does not erase files you previously exported or shared, nor copies retained in Apple-managed backups. Manage those separately using the relevant services' settings.

CareLog does not currently provide a separate cloud synchronization or full-data restoration service. If a device is lost, replaced, or reset, records may be unrecoverable unless a compatible system backup is available. Do not uninstall CareLog as a troubleshooting step without considering the risk of losing locally stored information.

Children's privacy

CareLog is intended for adult caregivers and families and is not designed for independent use by children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CareLog changes. Material changes will be reflected in the policy text and its last-updated date.

Contact

For questions about privacy or data handling, contact carelogfamily@gmail.com. Please do not send sensitive medical records in your initial message.

CareLog organizes information you enter. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical decisions.
